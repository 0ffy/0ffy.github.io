The price of an option is quoted on a per share basis, for example 5.5 USD. To buy the option, you have to pay 5.5 x 100 = 550 USD. Databases

SQL A database is a software for storing data. There are many many databases available. They can be broadly classified into two categories: one is relational database, including MySQL and PostgreSQL. Generative Models

Deep generative models concern the problem of finding high dimensional data distribution, and new data generation. There are four major types of generative models in the literature: generative adversarial networks (GANs) ( Citation: Goodfellow, Pouget-Abadie & al., 2014 Goodfellow, I., Pouget-Abadie, J., Mirza, M., Xu, B., Warde-Farley, D., Ozair, S., Courville, A. & Bengio, Y. NLP Models

A language model is a model that assigns probabilities to words and sentences. The probabilities should reflect word statistics in the training data. Probabilities can be very useful for many applications, e.g. for grammar correction, $$ \mathbb{P}(\text{This }\textit{means}\text{ in particular}) > \mathbb{P}(\text{This }\textit{mean}\text{ in particular}). $$ For model evaluation, extrinsic evaluation refers to performance measurements in real-world tasks like spelling correction or speech recognition. Intrinsic evaluation refers to calculating probabilities on some test set, to see which model outputs larger numbers. The common metric is the perplexity.
